ReconcileMyFiles Support

Compare two folders, decide what happens to each file, then apply it in one go.

How it works

ReconcileMyFiles compares two folders side by side. The left column is one folder, the right column is the other, and the middle column is where you say what should happen to each row. Nothing is written to disk while you work — your choices are queued and only carried out when you press Apply.

  1. Choose a folder on each side — use the Choose buttons, or drag a folder onto either box.
  2. Press Compare. The top level appears straight away.
  3. Go through the rows and click the action you want in the middle column.
  4. Press Apply and confirm. The activity log at the bottom records every operation.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to delete files with this app?
Deletions move items to the Trash rather than removing them permanently, so a mistake is recoverable. You're always asked to confirm before anything is applied, and told how many items will go to the Trash.

How are files compared?
By size and modification date, with a one-second tolerance. Contents are never hashed, so a file edited and saved back to the same size and timestamp will read as identical.

Why is a folder still showing a "?" status?
Large folders are scanned in the background — the top level appears immediately, and everything below arrives as it's read. A "?" folder just hasn't been scanned yet; it can still be sent to the Trash, but it can't be queued for a copy until its contents are known.

Comparing a big project folder is slow — can I speed it up?
Turn on Options ▸ Skip build folders (on by default), which ignores node_modules, DerivedData, Pods, .venv, and similar — usually the difference between seconds and minutes on a repository.

Does the app remember my last two folders?
No. The app starts fresh every launch, and choosing or swapping a folder clears the current comparison so you never act on a stale result.

What do the auto-decide options do?
Newer file wins gives each side the newer copy and copies across anything missing, without deleting anything. Mirror left → right and Mirror right → left make one side match the other exactly, including deleting what only exists on the destination side. Rows marked Skip are left alone by all three — always review the queue before applying, since mirroring deletes.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • ⌘R — Compare (or compare again)
  • ⌘⏎ — Apply the queued actions
  • ⌘? — Open the in-app guide
  • ⌘W — Close the window

For the full walkthrough, open the in-app guide from Help ▸ ReconcileMyFiles Help (⌘?).
