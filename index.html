Privacy Policy for ReconcileMyFiles

Last updated: September 21, 2026

ReconcileMyFiles is a macOS application that compares and reconciles two folders you select on your own Mac.

Data collection

ReconcileMyFiles does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no network access, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. It never sends information to us or to anyone else.

File access

ReconcileMyFiles only reads and writes files in folders that you explicitly choose using the macOS file picker. It runs inside Apple's App Sandbox, which limits it to the folders you select. All file operations happen locally on your device and never leave your Mac.

Third parties

ReconcileMyFiles does not share data with any third party, because it does not collect or transmit any data in the first place.

Children's privacy

ReconcileMyFiles does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
